# Frozen paper scope and materials

The current manuscript is `paper/matching_dilation.tex`: **Complementary-subset dilations and a spectral hierarchy for hafnian energies**. This replaces the earlier plan to give equal prominence to the entire normalized-model research history.

## Main text

The manuscript proceeds from the explicit weighted-operator dilation to the critical hafnian recursion, then to averaged energies by interlacing and counting. The Roos hybrid includes an explicit odd-ambient-dimension justification. The fixed-N density proposition covers both parities. The full six-variable PSD equality proof and a carefully delimited real collision-free GBS corollary complete the paper.

The argument does not depend on the original cube-normalized 1/216 conjecture, experimental performance claims, or an unproved mixed-kernel positivity condition. The complete all-orders equality classification and sharp fixed-N constants beyond the critical size are future questions, not missing steps in the stated theorems.
