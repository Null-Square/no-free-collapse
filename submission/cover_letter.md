Dear Editors of Linear Algebra and its Applications,

Please consider the manuscript “Complementary-subset dilations and a spectral hierarchy for hafnian energies” for publication.

The paper presents an explicit complementary-subset dilation for a matrix-weighted Kneser operator. It gives a sharp spectral-diameter bound in every even dimension and yields a recurrence for averaged squared principal subhafnians whenever the ambient order satisfies N >= 4r-2. We prove sharpness at each critical size, combine the recurrence with Roos's published inequalities, and identify the exact fixed-dimension spectral-density ceiling. A complete six-dimensional PSD equality theorem and a restricted collision-free real Gaussian-boson-sampling corollary are included.

The contribution is a spectral matrix inequality and its consequences, not a claimed computational speedup or a solution of the separate cube-normalized hafnian extremal problem. Proofs are analytic; the accompanying code contains exact rational certificates and separately labeled numerical diagnostics.
