# Literature comparison and priority-review record

Checked 12 September 2026. This log distinguishes a direct proposition comparison from an exhaustive priority search; it does not certify novelty by absence of search results.

## Roos — closest direct comparator

B. Roos, New inequalities for permanents and hafnians and some generalizations, Linear and Multilinear Algebra 73 (2025), 1634-1667; DOI 10.1080/03081087.2024.2436061; preprint https://arxiv.org/html/1906.06176v2 . Theorem 2.3 gives log-subadditivity of exactly the normalized energies G_r. Corollary 3.1 supplies the parity-free subset-convolution inequality, and the new manuscript explicitly derives the odd-N application. Our new step uses d(A)^2/[4(2r-1)^2] rather than G_1. At critical matching projections the ratio of those coefficients is (2r-1)^2/(4r-3). This is a strict comparison against that specified bound, not a claim to outperform every inequality in the literature.

## Weighted versus ordinary Kneser operators

M. Kummer, Spectral linear matrix inequalities, Adv. Math. 384 (2021), 107749; DOI 10.1016/j.aim.2021.107749; https://arxiv.org/html/2008.13452v2 . Lemma 3.5 proves a square-free derivative isomorphism using ordinary Kneser adjacency; Theorem 2.2 concerns spectrahedral lifting. Those displayed statements do not state our weighted spectral-diameter inequality. A specialist should still check possible indirect implications.

K. R. Davidson and A. P. Donsig, Norms of Schur multipliers, Illinois J. Math. 51 (2007), DOI 10.1215/ijm/1258131101; https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0506073 . Section 5 studies Kneser/Johnson adjacency patterns. Theorem 5.5 computes a Schur multiplier norm for the adjacency of K(2n+1,n), rather than the operator norm of the matrix-weighted complement-edge construction here. The section and theorem were examined; equality of graph vocabulary alone does not imply equality of theorems.

P. Feinsilver, Representations of sl(2) in the Boolean lattice, and the Hamming and Johnson schemes, Infinite Dimensional Analysis, Quantum Probability and Related Topics 15(3) (2012), 1250019; DOI 10.1142/S0219025712500191; https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0368 . This is established Boolean-lattice/zeon representation background, not a new contribution of the present paper.
